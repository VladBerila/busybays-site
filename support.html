Playing

How do I play?

Tap a bus. If nothing is in its way, it drives out of the lot to a free loading bay. Riders at the front of the line board any parked bus of their color, and a full bus leaves and frees its bay. You lose a level when every open bay is taken and nobody at the front of the line can board.

Is every level winnable?

Yes. Every level is built so it can be cleared with the bays it gives you, and this is checked automatically for every level. Hard levels and boss levels are marked on the map before you start.

What are the grey buses with a question mark?

Mystery buses. They show their color once their road is clear.

What do the boosters do?

Extra Bay opens one more loading bay (you can also tap a locked bay). Smart Shuffle recolors the buses that can leave to match the riders who are waiting. Airlift lifts any bus, even a buried one, straight to a bay.

Lives

You have up to five lives, and one comes back every 20 minutes. You only lose a life by giving up on a level or leaving it after you've made a move. Leaving before your first move is free. Closing the app in the middle of a level counts as leaving it.

Purchases

I bought something and didn't get it.

Close the game completely and open it again: the App Store sometimes confirms a purchase a little later, and the game picks it up at launch. If it's still missing, write to me with the date of the purchase.

I have a new phone. Can I get my purchases back?

No Ads and the Starter Pack's No Ads come back with Settings → Restore Purchases. Coins, and the boosters and unlimited lives you buy with them, are used up as you play, so the App Store cannot restore them, and progress is saved only on the device you play on.

Refunds

All payments go through Apple, so refunds do too: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

You can choose to watch a short video for free coins, a revive, a booster, a life, or double rewards. After level 7, an ad may also appear between levels, at most once every three levels won. No Ads (or the Starter Pack) removes the ads between levels; the optional videos stay available because you choose to watch them.

If a video doesn't load, check your connection and try again in a moment.

Privacy

To stop the game sending gameplay statistics and crash reports, switch off Settings → Share gameplay statistics. The full details are in the privacy policy.
